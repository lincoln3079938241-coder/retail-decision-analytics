# Retail Decision Analytics

A reproducible decision case over 2.6M transaction lines linking customer profiles, pre-campaign value, observed response ranking, category mix, basket affinities, and short-horizon forecasting. A household-grouped holdout produced 0.826 ROC-AUC and 3.79× top-decile observed-response lift. Value tiers built strictly from pre-campaign behavior showed 7.78%/12.57%/16.65% observed response, with household-cluster bootstrap intervals. Segment/category indices add product-mix context without claiming incrementality. The strongest basket pair had 2.62 lift, and Seasonal Naive led forecasting at 9.30% WAPE.
